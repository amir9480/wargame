## 🎮 Play

**[▶ Launch the game](https://YOUR_USER.github.io/wargame/)** – nothing to download or install, it runs right in your browser.

For the best experience use a desktop browser with hardware acceleration enabled (Chrome, Edge or Firefox) and a reasonably recent graphics card. Press **F11** for fullscreen.

## 🗺️ The battlefield

Every match is played on a freshly generated map – no two skirmishes look alike.

- 🏔️ Mountain ranges, snow-capped peaks, rivers, coastlines and open plains
- 🏙️ Cities with street grids, traffic lights and street lamps
- 🌉 Roads, railways, tunnels and bridges you can use to move your army
- ⚡ Power plants, substations and pylon lines crossing the countryside
- 🌗 A full day–night cycle with real shadows, lamps and glowing windows

## ⚔️ How to play

You command the **blue** team against **three AI commanders**. Build a base, grow an economy, raise an army and **destroy every enemy structure** to win.

Pick a difficulty – **Easy**, **Normal** or **Hard** – and start the skirmish.

### Your base

| Building | Role |
|---|---|
| 🏛️ **Command Center** | Your headquarters and a steady trickle of income. Lose them all and you're out. |
| ⚡ **Power Plant** | Generates the power your other buildings need. |
| 📦 **Supply Depot** | Big credit income. Mind its power draw. |
| 🏕️ **Barracks** | Trains infantry. |
| 🏭 **War Factory** | Builds trucks and heavy armour. |
| 🔫 **MG Turret** | Cheap automated base defence. |

### Your army

| Unit | Role |
|---|---|
| 🪖 **Rifleman** | Cheap, fast, all-round infantry. |
| 🚀 **Rocket Trooper** | Longer range and harder hitting. |
| 🚚 **Supply Truck** | Fast wheeled logistics. |
| 🛡️ **Battle Tank** | Tough, long-range armour – the backbone of any assault. |

> 💡 **Tip:** keep your power supply ahead of demand – several buildings draw power, and Power Plants provide it.

## 🕹️ Controls

| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| Move the camera | `W` `A` `S` `D` / arrow keys, or push the mouse to a screen edge |
| Rotate the camera | `Q` / `E`, or hold the **middle mouse button** and drag |
| Zoom | Mouse wheel |
| Select units | Left-click, or drag a box |
| Order units | Right-click |
| Control groups | `Ctrl` + `1`–`9` to set, `1`–`9` to recall |
| Jump to your base | `H` |
| Sell selected building | `Delete` |
| Cancel / pause menu | `Esc` |
| Pause | `P` |
| Fullscreen | `F11` |

## 🏆 Winning

Destroy every structure belonging to all three enemy commanders before they do the same to you.
