# Content layers (`src/render/`)

- **Ground** (`ground.ts`): terrain chunks, water, edge skirt, data textures (half-float heights = water depth, RG8 cover: R dirt, G forest canopy). Chunks are built only when their heights changed and later rewritten in place (fixed topology). Water: `STREAM` define for mountain streams (own surface height, ripples, foam where steep, hidden beyond 900 m). `WATER_LEVEL = 0`, snow above `SNOW_LINE` (shared with rock shader).
- **Roads** (`roads.ts`): ribbons (3 columns, ragged alpha edges, asphalt lane markings, round caps at angled joins, asphalt over dirt via `renderOrder`); rail = same ribbon with `RAIL` define (ballast, sleepers, steel rails), drawn last; at level crossings only the rails cross the road (`railClips`); own bridge style. Per type + bridges via `steps()`. Bases are draped translucent squares with borders (`bases.ts`). Tunnel portals: `tunnels.ts` via `UrbanLayer`.
- **Road paint** (centre dashes, edge lines, crosswalks, stop lines) is computed in `road.frag.glsl` from per-vertex attributes (no paint meshes); fades 140–220 m and is skipped beyond.
- **Street lights** (`streetlights.ts`, plan in `terrain/streetlights.ts`): asphalt = tall steel poles every 24 m alternating sides (spot light, warm white), dirt = wooden post with hanging lantern (point light); one seeded colour per street (`streetIds`); car-park lamps. One InstancedMesh per chunk and lamp type (`lodMax` 800), switched by daylight (`update(daylight)`: lamp emissive + light dimmer).
- **Buildings** (`buildings/layer.ts`, `geometry.ts`): per chunk one near mesh (`lodMax` = `buildingDistance`: window panes + sills, doors, all details, `aWin` = [kind, lit]) and one far mesh (`lodMin`, casts shadows at its range: boxes + roofs, windows drawn by `buildingFar.frag` from `aFacade`/`aGrid` with a glow around lit ones), far window halo discs (`buildingHalo.vert` + `lighthalo.frag`) and a `LightGroup` for gas canopies. **Windows** are identical in both levels (`windowLit(key, seed, share)` in `chunks/windows.glsl`: never lit (1 − litShare), on 17:30–20:30 ±0.5 h, off 21:30–01:30 or at dawn 05:00–07:00; also scaled by `lampLevel`; flat varyings). Roof decks sit inside parapets (`ROOF_INSET`). Abandoned = broken top floor, dropped roof, dark/boarded windows, debris. Construction (`buildSite`): concrete frame, slabs, crane from `Building.crane` (near adds columns, rebar, lattice mast, cab, hook; far boxes; no windows/halos). **Destruction** is CPU-side: each building's body and rubble are vertex ranges of its chunk buffers; `BuildingState` changes collapse/restore them (`addUpdateRange`) so the shadow pass agrees. Street lights keep 1.2 m from footprints.
- **UrbanLayer** (`urban.ts`): ground decals (`decal.*.glsl`: flat grids following the ground, same depth bias as roads, renderOrder 0.95, `noShadow`): paving round town buildings, asphalt forecourts under gas stations and car parks (bays, kerb, entrance, driveways `toRoad`), parked cars (`lodMax`), traffic lights (InstancedMesh per chunk, `aSignal` = [group, offset], lamps lit from `time`, `lodMax` = lightDistance / 2), signal point lights in a `createGroup(true)` group.
- **City models = third LOD of a town** (`cityLod.ts` `CityLodLayer`, `townLod.ts` `TownLod`, quality `cityDistance` 450/600/780/950 m): beyond that camera distance to the town's *edge* (`distance` + radius × `TOWN_REACH` from the centre, so the whole town switches at once) its detailed content is hidden and **two draw calls** per town remain: ONE merged mesh (buildings' `buildFar` shapes without window grid, drawn with the plain `building.*` shaders; street surfaces as flat strips = sidewalk strip + narrower asphalt/dirt strip draped on the road heights, `LIFT_*` over ground; paved yards / forecourts / car parks as quads; no lamps, signals, cars, paint; `polygonOffset`; casts shadows) and ONE instanced halo mesh (`cityhalo.vert.glsl`: every town street lamp, steady, + one disc per lit building on the window schedule; `aHaloShare < 0` = lamp; an instance that hasn't faded in yet — `lampRange` / `buildingRange` uniforms — is moved out of the clip volume in the vertex shader, so near ones cost nothing). Content is hidden by `TownLod.tag(mesh, city)` → Culler `lodMax` + `lodMaxCenter` (the town centre); the model uses role `model` (`lodMin` + `lodCenter`). Tagged: building far meshes + window halos (`BuildingLayer` splits each chunk's buildings per town: key `chunk + (town + 1) * TOWN_KEY`), town triangles of road ribbons / pavement (`splitByChunk(geo, grid, classify)` puts the class in `geometry.userData.group`) and paving / forecourt / car-park decals. Near buildings, lamp posts, signals and cars already end sooner (`lodMax` 380 / 425). Street lamps in a town carry `LightDef.noHalo` (their disc comes from the city model; `StreetLightLayer.halos` feeds it), traffic-signal halos end at `cityDistance` (`LightGroup.setHaloMax`). Destruction: the model keeps each building's body / rubble vertex range (`show`, exported from `buildings/layer.ts`) and its halo instance, `BuildingLayer.apply` forwards to `CityLodLayer.setDestroyed`. Built in the `lights` job right after the street lights. New town content that is drawn by chunk: split it per town, tag it, and decide whether the model needs a simple version of it.
- **Landmarks** (`landmarks.ts`): lighthouses (banded tapering tower, gallery, lantern room; two rotating spot-ray beams + steady glow, on from dusk to dawn) and telecom/radar towers (lattice mast, platforms, panel antennas, cabin; blinking red beacons, always on, brighter at night). Each is a near + far world-space mesh pair (`NEAR_DISTANCE` 450); lights via animated `LightGroup`s.
- **Power** (`power.ts`): see skill `terrain-power`.
- Rocks (`rocks.ts`, 4 low-poly shapes, moss/snow sampled only where shown) and trees (`trees.ts`: pine / broadleaf, wind sway) are instanced per chunk with sprite far LOD (`billboard.ts`).
