# Lighting (`lighting.ts`, `shaders/post.*`)

- Scene renders into a half-float target with a depth texture (no MSAA on any preset; FXAA in the final pass). One full-screen pass rebuilds world pos from depth and applies a 2048² sun shadow map (ortho, texel-snapped). Casters are culled by the sun frustum but at the **main camera's LOD levels** (`World.cullShadow(lightCamera, camera)`); sprites/water/decals flagged `userData.noShadow`; far trees / rocks cast through low-poly `userData.shadowOnly` stand-ins (`shadowProxy` in billboard.ts). 14-step contact shadows and SSAO, then sRGB output. Shadow share = sun/(ambient+sun) from depth-derived normals (over ≥ one terrain cell), so material shaders are unchanged.
- **Shadow map cache** (`Lighting.shadowCache`): redrawn only when the sun moved > `SHADOW_SUN_STEP` (~0.25°), the texel-snapped light view / region changed (4 m steps), the culling camera moved > `SHADOW_CAMERA_STEP` (6 m), or `World.shadowEpoch` changed; quality changes invalidate it. Anything that changes what casts (moving casters, LOD switches not tied to the camera) must bump the epoch.
- **SSAO** (`ao.frag.glsl`, `Lighting.renderAO`): half resolution, 3 passes (linear depth R32F → occlusion with compile-time `AO_TAPS`, 4x4 interleaved rotation → depth-aware blur). Post pass upsamples with depth-aware bilinear weights. Effects fade beyond a region round the camera focus (radius 0.45 × zoom, 130–200 m). Pixel ratio capped per preset. Contact shadows only where the shadow map left the pixel lit, within 140 m (faded from 60 m).
- **Soft shadows**: `textures/shadow_noise.png` (64×64 blue noise from `generate-textures.ts`: rg = cos/sin angle, b = value) fetched once per pixel; shadow map read at 2 taps (4 on ultra) along that direction, each a hardware 2×2 PCF. The same fetch jitters AO / contact shadows.
- **Day/night** (`timeOfDay.ts`): panel slider sets the hour (0–24, default 9). `applyTimeOfDay` mutates the shared `Environment` in place (sun, ambient, fog, `daylight`). Sun rises east at 6, sets west at 18, stays north of overhead. Sun below the horizon ⇒ shadow map and contact shadows skipped. Far-LOD sprites carry baked shading: `World.refreshSprites()` re-bakes when the sun moved ~0.6° or daylight changed 0.02, one pitch column per frame. Sky / aerial LUTs re-bake at ~0.3°; the colour-grade LUT (`gradeLut.ts`) builds a few slices per frame.

## Local lights (`lights.ts`, `shaders/light.*`)
- **Light level switches are dithered cross-fades, never hard cuts** (`chunks/townfade.glsl` + `townkeep.glsl`, same blue noise as the trees / city model): town lights fade detailed <-> city model by the **3D** distance fogOrigin -> town centre (`TownLod.info`: 2 vec4 per town; the Culler and `buildingFar` measure in 3D too, so xz-only distances desync), non-town lights fade deferred volume <-> overlay pool per light around `deferredDistance` (`lightSwitch` uniform, `LIGHT_BAND`; `LIGHT_SLACK` in lights.ts must stay >= band + half a chunk diagonal because culling is per chunk).
- `LightField` (`World.lights`) holds `LightGroup`s of point/spot `LightDef`s. **Never add ad-hoc `THREE.PointLight`/`SpotLight` or a private glow**: `field.createGroup(signal?, animated?)`, `group.build(defs, chunkGrid)`, `group.level` (0–1 dimmer; 0 = skipped), `group.clear()`. One InstancedMesh of light-volume spheres per chunk (frustum + distance culled by its own `Culler`), drawn additively into a half-float light buffer (`lightResolution` share of the screen); each pixel rebuilds position/normal from depth and writes irradiance; the post pass upsamples depth-aware and multiplies with the surface colour after sun shadows. No shadows from local lights; needs the post pass (low quality draws overlay quads: camera-facing glow + a pool draped over the terrain, `LightGroup.buildFake`). **Deferred volumes are for close lights only, at every quality**: `field.setDeferredDistance(lightDistance × 0.5)`; beyond it the same cheap overlay pools take over (`LightGroup.applyDistances`: rural volumes `lodMax` = distance + 20, pools `lodMin` = distance − 20; low quality has no volumes and pools everywhere). Pools are in the main scene, so `field.cullOverlay(camera)` runs before the scene draw (and `fakeGroup` is hidden while the shadow map is drawn); `field.cull` handles halos + volumes. **Town lights** (`LightDef.city` = town index, set by street lights, signals, gas canopies) follow their town: their volumes are tagged detail (`TownLod`, gone beyond the town's city-model distance), their chunk pools only exist in low quality (detail too), and once the town's city model is drawn ALL its lights' pools come as ONE pool mesh per town (`buildTownPools`, role `model`) at every quality. **In the cross-fade band the two never add up**: every town light carries `aTown` (town index + 1 = detailed, negative = city model; 0 = none) and the shared `townInfo[8]` uniform (`TownLod.info`: centre, switch distance, band) feeds `townFade()` (`chunks/townfade.glsl`), which scales its brightness by 1 − share or share, share being the same distance smoothstep as the model's dither. Applied in `light.vert` (volumes), `lightfake.vert` (pools), `buildingHalo.vert` (detailed window halos) and `cityhalo.vert` (model window halos; lamp halos have no detailed twin). New town light kinds need the attribute + uniform.
- **Light halos** (`lighthalo.*`, `field.haloGroup`, drawn at every quality): one disc per light, size `max(radius·0.12, dist·0.012)`, fading in between `haloStart/haloEnd` (`group.setHaloRange(start, end)`; street / traffic lights 0.08×–0.35× of the lamp draw distance, gas canopies 0.1×–0.4×, windows 0.12×–0.5×, landmarks / power 0.1×–0.45×) so lit things stay visible when models are culled. Set the range so the halo is at full strength before the near model is culled. Emissive things that aren't a `LightDef` (per-window lights) need their own halo instances with the same fade (`buildingHalo.vert`). `LightDef.noHalo` = this group draws no disc for it (town street lamps: the town's city model draws them in one instanced mesh, `cityhalo.vert`, see `render-content-layers`); `LightGroup.setHaloMax(d)` ends a group's discs at a camera distance (traffic signals at `cityDistance`).
- `LightDef` extras: `dir/angle` (spot), `groundY`, `facing`, `signal: [group, offset]` (traffic clock colour; group made with `createGroup(true)`, white `color`), `anim: { kind: 'blink' | 'rotate', period, phase, duty }` (group made with `createGroup(false, true)`, `chunks/anim.glsl`), `shaft`, `rayLength`, `haloSize`, `rayOnly`.
- **Light rays** (high/ultra, `ray.*`): per spot light an instanced bounding sphere in `LightField.shaftScene` (chunk-culled by `cullShafts`) drawn into a `lightResolution` target; each pixel marches the camera ray (6 steps, stopped by scene depth) through the cone; added as is by the post pass.
- **Wet-ground reflections** (`lightReflections`, `reflect.*`): one screen-space streak per bright thing below it, only where `wetmask.ts` (R8, 0.5 m texels) says the ground mirrors (asphalt, car parks, forecourts 1; sidewalks, paved yards less); close-up only (90–150 m). `ReflectionGroup`s: every `LightGroup` has one; non-`LightDef` lit things use `field.createReflections(defines, uniforms)` + `add(spots, lodCenter, extraAttributes)` (buildings: `WINDOW` define, `glowSpots`).

## Graphics quality (`quality.ts`)
Panel select low/normal/high/ultra (default high; `engine.quality`; persisted). One `QualitySettings` preset drives render scale + pixel-ratio cap (high 1.25, ultra 1.5), the post pass on/off, shadow-map size, shadow taps (1/2/4), contact-shadow steps, AO taps, deferred vs overlay lights, `lightShafts`, `lightReflections`, `bokeh`, billboard (sprite) distance, `terrainLodScale`, light / lamp-post / building distance and texture anisotropy. `low` renders at 75% straight to the screen (no post pass: no shadows/AO). New cost-vs-looks knobs belong in `QualitySettings`.
